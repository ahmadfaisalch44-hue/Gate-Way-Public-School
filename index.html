About Us

Our Legacy

Established in 1994, Gateway Public High School has been a beacon of academic excellence and character building. For decades, we have been committed to providing an enriching educational environment that fosters intellectual, physical, and ethical growth in our students, preparing them for the challenges of the modern world.

Academic Structure & Levels

Primary Level

  • Playgroup to Grade 5
  • Activity-based learning framework
  • Focus on basic literacy & numeracy
  • Moral & character building programs

Middle Level

  • Grade 6 to Grade 8
  • Core focus on Science & Mathematics
  • Computer Education & IT Skills
  • Analytical thinking development

High School (Matric)

  • Science Group (Biology / Computer Science)
  • Affiliated with BISE Lahore Board
  • Exam-oriented preparation & tests
  • Practical lab work in Science & IT

Facilities & Methodology

Modern Science & IT Labs

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Computer laboratories to give students real practical experience.
